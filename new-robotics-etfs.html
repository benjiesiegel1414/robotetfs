The big themes behind the launches

Humanoids. Most of the new funds target humanoid robots and the parts inside them. $KOID has grown into the largest of the group, while funds like $HUMN, $BOTT, $HOID and $CBOT take their own angle on the same idea.

Robot parts. $TORK and $AT focus on actuators and motion components, the pieces that let a robot move.

Big-name entrants. $RBTX brings the largest ETF issuer in the world into active robotics investing.

Physical AI and autonomy. $WDRN mixes humanoids and drones, $CABZ targets robotaxis and $DRNZ focuses on drones.

What to check before buying a new fund

  • Assets: very small funds can close if they do not attract money.
  • Trading: check the bid-ask spread and use limit orders.
  • Holdings: many new funds overlap heavily with each other.
  • Fees: compare against older funds like $BOTZ and $ROBO.

Frequently asked questions

What is the newest robotics ETF?

The fund at the top of the table above is the most recent launch on our list. We add new funds as they start trading.

Why are so many robotics ETFs launching?

Interest in humanoid robots, physical AI and robotaxis surged, and fund companies launched products to meet that demand.

Are new ETFs riskier?

New funds have no track record, are often small and can have wider trading spreads. Some new ETFs close if they do not gather assets.
