What counts as ai & robotics

These funds pair robotics with the wider AI trade: chip designers, cloud platforms and software companies sit next to robot and automation makers.

They are usually larger and less volatile than pure robotics funds, and they overlap more with broad technology ETFs. If you want robots specifically, check how much of each fund is really robotics.

Each fund in plain English

1. $AIQ: Global X Artificial Intelligence & Technology ETF

Global X · Index · $9.40B in assets · 0.68% expense ratio · launched May 11, 2018

Global X's AI and technology ETF is the largest fund on our list. It tracks companies developing and using AI, including the chip, cloud and software giants plus robotics and automation names.

Worth knowing: This is a broad AI fund rather than a pure robotics fund. It holds many of the same mega-cap tech stocks found in a Nasdaq index fund.

Full $AIQ profile and live price →

2. $ARTY: iShares Future AI & Tech ETF

iShares (BlackRock) · Index · $3.30B in assets · 0.47% expense ratio · launched June 26, 2018

The iShares Future AI & Tech ETF tracks the Morningstar Global Artificial Intelligence Select Index. It used to trade as IRBO, the iShares Robotics and AI fund, before its mandate changed in 2024. Today it is mostly AI chips, cloud and software, led by names such as AMD, Broadcom and TSMC.

Worth knowing: This is an AI fund now, not a robotics fund. Do not expect much industrial robot or humanoid exposure. We list it because many investors still look for it under its old robotics name.

Full $ARTY profile and live price →

3. $ROBT: First Trust Nasdaq Artificial Intelligence and Robotics ETF

First Trust · Index · $696M in assets · 0.65% expense ratio · launched February 21, 2018

First Trust's Nasdaq AI and robotics ETF tracks companies classified as AI and robotics enablers, engagers and enhancers, with a fairly equal-weighted approach.

Worth knowing: Its equal weighting spreads money across many mid-size names. That limits mega-cap concentration but can lag when the biggest AI stocks lead.

Full $ROBT profile and live price →

4. $THNQ: ROBO Global Artificial Intelligence ETF

Exchange Traded Concepts (ROBO Global) · Index · $418M in assets · 0.68% expense ratio · launched May 11, 2020

ROBO Global's AI ETF tracks companies across the AI value chain, from chips and cloud infrastructure to AI software and robotics applications.

Worth knowing: A companion fund to ROBO with more of a software and infrastructure tilt. Check overlap if you own both.

Full $THNQ profile and live price →

5. $LOUP: Innovator Deepwater Frontier Tech ETF

Innovator · Index · $173M in assets · 0.70% expense ratio · launched July 24, 2018

Innovator's Deepwater frontier tech ETF tracks a concentrated list of frontier technology companies chosen by Deepwater Asset Management, covering AI, robotics, autonomy, AR and VR.

Worth knowing: Concentrated and volatile. Robotics is one of several frontier themes inside it, not the only focus.

Full $LOUP profile and live price →

6. $IQM: Franklin Intelligent Machines ETF

Franklin Templeton · Active · $70M in assets · 0.50% expense ratio · launched February 25, 2020

Franklin's intelligent machines ETF is an actively managed fund investing in companies behind intelligent machines: robotics, automation, sensors and the chips and software that power them.

Worth knowing: It often holds large positions in AI chip and semiconductor equipment names, so it can trade much like a tech growth fund. It pays little or no dividend.

Full $IQM profile and live price →

7. $PBOT: Pictet AI & Automation ETF

Pictet · $15M in assets · 0.70% expense ratio · launched October 15, 2025

Pictet's AI and automation ETF brings the Swiss asset manager's thematic investing to US investors with a focus on AI-driven automation and robotics.

Worth knowing: A newer, smaller fund. Compare its holdings with broad AI ETFs to see how much is truly robotics.
