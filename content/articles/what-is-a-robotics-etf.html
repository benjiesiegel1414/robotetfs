A robotics ETF is a fund that owns a basket of companies building robots and automation. Instead of picking one robot maker, you buy a single ticker and get exposure to dozens of companies across the supply chain.

What is inside a robotics ETF

Most robotics funds mix several groups of companies:

  • Robot makers: industrial robot arms, collaborative robots, surgical robots and warehouse robots.
  • Components: actuators, reducers, motors, bearings, sensors and machine vision.
  • Brains: AI chips, simulation software and industrial software that plan and control robots.
  • Users and integrators: companies that design automated factories and logistics systems.

Five things to check before buying

  1. Purity: how much of the fund is truly robotics versus general tech.
  2. Concentration: how much sits in the top 10 holdings.
  3. Geography: many robotics leaders are in Japan, China, Korea and Europe, which adds currency and country risk.
  4. Size and trading: small funds can have wide spreads and may close.
  5. Fees: fees compound against you every year.
