How to choose between them

Decide how pure you want it. Funds like {{link:BOTZ}}, {{link:ROBO}} and {{link:IBOT}} stay close to robotics and automation. Funds like {{link:AIQ}} and {{link:THNQ}} are broader AI funds where robotics is one slice next to chips, cloud and software.

Decide how early you want to be. Humanoid funds like {{link:KOID}}, {{link:HUMN}} and {{link:BOTT}} bet on a market that barely exists yet. Many of their holdings are component makers whose revenue still comes mostly from cars, factories and electronics.

Check size and trading. Many of the newest funds hold only a few million dollars. Small funds can have wide bid-ask spreads and a higher chance of closing.

Watch the overlap. Big AI chip and automation names show up in many of these funds. Owning three robotics ETFs may not be as diversified as it looks.

Know what leverage does. {{link:UBOT}} seeks 2X the daily return of its index and resets daily. It is a trading tool, not a long-term holding for most investors.
