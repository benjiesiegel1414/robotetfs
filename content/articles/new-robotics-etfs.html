The big themes behind the launches

Humanoids. Most of the new funds target humanoid robots and the parts inside them. {{link:KOID}} has grown into the largest of the group, while funds like {{link:HUMN}}, {{link:BOTT}}, {{link:HOID}} and {{link:CBOT}} take their own angle on the same idea.

Robot parts. {{link:TORK}} and {{link:AT}} focus on actuators and motion components, the pieces that let a robot move.

Big-name entrants. {{link:RBTX}} brings the largest ETF issuer in the world into active robotics investing.

Physical AI and autonomy. {{link:WDRN}} mixes humanoids and drones, {{link:CABZ}} targets robotaxis and {{link:DRNZ}} focuses on drones.

What to check before buying a new fund

  • Assets: very small funds can close if they do not attract money.
  • Trading: check the bid-ask spread and use limit orders.
  • Holdings: many new funds overlap heavily with each other.
  • Fees: compare against older funds like {{link:BOTZ}} and {{link:ROBO}}.
