The short version

$AIQ is the bigger fund, with $10.0B in assets. $AIQ has been around longer, launching on May 11, 2018.

$AIQ

Global X's AI and technology ETF is the largest fund on our list. It tracks companies developing and using AI, including the chip, cloud and software giants plus robotics and automation names. This is a broad AI fund rather than a pure robotics fund. It holds many of the same mega-cap tech stocks found in a Nasdaq index fund.

$THNQ

ROBO Global's AI ETF tracks companies across the AI value chain, from chips and cloud infrastructure to AI software and robotics applications. A companion fund to ROBO with more of a software and infrastructure tilt. Check overlap if you own both.

Lower fees and more assets are helpful, but they are not the whole story. The bigger question is which strategy matches what you want to own. This comparison is not a recommendation to buy or sell either fund.

FAQ

What is the difference between AIQ and THNQ?

AIQ is the Global X Artificial Intelligence & Technology ETF from Global X (ai & robotics), while THNQ is the ROBO Global Artificial Intelligence ETF from Exchange Traded Concepts (ROBO Global) (ai & robotics). They differ in strategy, fees and size.

Which is cheaper, AIQ or THNQ?

They charge the same expense ratio, or data is not available.

Which is bigger, AIQ or THNQ?

AIQ has more assets: $10.0B vs $484M.
